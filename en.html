What is Nagnebang?

We share a room.

Nagnebang offers a safe place to live for young adults who need a home base in the city. By sharing the most essential thing — a place to live — we help people build the foundation to live their own lives.

No contracts. No deposit. Residents contribute 10% of their income as a house fee, share common spaces, and live at their own pace. The food in the fridge, the things in the living room — everything is shared.

We do this because everyone, at some point in life, needs somewhere to lean. Living alongside others makes us better people — and that's more than enough reason. This isn't about sacrifice. It's simply a better way to live.

❶
Financial

The operator covers the full deposit. Residents pay 10% of their income as a house fee. If you have no income, the minimum is ₩200,000/month — and in genuine hardship, contributions in kind are welcome.

❷
Daily Life

Basic furniture and appliances are provided. Common spaces and the shared fridge are for everyone. What we have, we share.

❸
Mindset

If someone else needs this room more than you, we ask that you gracefully make space. Nagnebang is not a place to stay forever — it's a place to prepare for what comes next.

❹
Community

We value loose but warm connections. Operators, residents, and supporters all look out for one another — even after the journey moves on.
